\section{Breaking Long Lines}
The maximum line length is \textbf{80 characters}. Lines longer than this should be split.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Break at Natural Points}: Break lines at natural points, such as after operators.
    \item \textbf{Indentation for Continuation Lines}: When breaking lines, ensure the continued line is indented properly.
\end{itemize}
