\subsection{Calendar Modifications}

The project calendar has been modified to reflect the team's actual work schedule:

\subsubsection{Academic Calendar Considerations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Fall 2025 Semester}: August 26 -- December 12, 2025
    \item \textbf{Winter Break}: December 13, 2025 -- January 12, 2026 (reduced availability)
    \item \textbf{Spring 2026 Semester}: January 13 -- May 15, 2026
    \item \textbf{Spring Break}: March 8-15, 2026 (no project work)
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Weekly Work Schedule}

The team operates on the following weekly schedule:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Work Days}: Monday through Friday
    \item \textbf{Weekend}: Saturday and Sunday (non-working)
    \item \textbf{Team Meetings}: Tuesday and Thursday, 5:00 PM -- 7:00 PM
    \item \textbf{Individual Work}: Distributed throughout week
    \item \textbf{Average Hours}: 8-10 hours per person per week during semester
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Impact on Schedule}

The modified calendar affects the schedule in the following ways:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Winter break (December 13, 2025 -- January 12, 2026) reduces team availability as members travel and formal meetings are suspended. Light asynchronous work such as documentation and planning continues during this period, with regular progress resuming January 13.

    \item Spring break represents 1 week of reduced project activity
    \item Final exams periods (December, May) have reduced team availability
    \item The Build phase is scheduled during Spring semester when lab access is maximized
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Resource Loading}

The Gantt chart includes resource assignments showing which team members are responsible for each task. This enables identification of:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Resource conflicts (multiple assignments on same dates)
    \item Underutilization periods
    \item Critical resource constraints
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Schedule Monitoring}

The Gantt chart serves as the primary schedule monitoring tool. Weekly progress reviews compare:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Planned dates vs. actual completion dates
    \item Percent complete for in-progress activities
    \item Impact of delays on downstream activities
    \item Critical path status and total float consumption
\end{itemize}

Schedule updates are recorded in the project management tool (GanttProject) and shared with the sponsor at bi-weekly status meetings.
