# Mechanical Designs - ESET 420 Capstone Submission

Mechanical design artifacts for the STAR robot chassis and its
simulation model.

## Authoritative Source

`STAR.f3z` (55 MB, Git LFS) -- Fusion 360 archive of the complete
chassis assembly. This is the authoritative editable source; all
mechanical geometry flows from here. Owner: Michael Norton.

`cad_README.md` -- the original `cad/README.md` from the top of the
repository, giving context on the Fusion 360 workflow and export
conventions.

Status: `[IMPLEMENTED]` -- f3z is the complete assembly.

## Neutral-Format STEP

Not staged in this directory. Exporting a neutral-format STEP file
from Fusion 360 is a manual workflow owned by the mechanical lead;
it is not generated by `kicad-cli` (which only covers electrical
design). The 3D model of the PCB itself is under
`final_docs/electrical_schematics_pcb/STAR_MCU_PCB.step`; pair it
with a Fusion 360 STEP export of the chassis when a full
chassis-plus-electronics assembly is required.

## Gazebo / URDF Model

The robot's simulation model is the practical consumer of the
mechanical design. Three artifacts are included:

- `star.urdf.xacro` -- primary URDF for Nav2 / SLAM runtime.
  Defines the base link, wheel joints, and sensor frames.
- `star_sim.urdf.xacro` -- simulation-only URDF used by the Gazebo
  SITL harness (`star_simulation` package). Inherits from the
  primary URDF and adds sensor plugins.
- `gazebo_meshes/` -- STL meshes referenced by both URDFs:
  - `chassis_visual.stl` -- rendered chassis geometry.
  - `chassis_collision.stl` -- simplified chassis collision hull.
  - `wheel_visual.stl` -- rendered wheel geometry.
  - `wheel_collision.stl` -- simplified wheel collision hull.

These are the mechanical design as the software actually sees it.
They are regenerated from the Fusion 360 model whenever the
chassis geometry changes.

Status: `[IMPLEMENTED]` -- URDFs and meshes are in production use
by the ROS2 simulation and Nav2 stack.

## Reviewer Reading Order

1. Open `STAR.f3z` in Fusion 360 (or any cloud viewer that reads
   the format) for the full chassis assembly.
2. Inspect `gazebo_meshes/chassis_visual.stl` in any STL viewer
   (MeshLab, Preview, Blender) for a quick outline of the
   physical footprint.
3. Read `star.urdf.xacro` for the joint tree, mass properties, and
   sensor frame transforms that the ROS2 stack consumes.
