QUICK TECHNICAL FACTS:

Hardware:
  - Operating range: 0.05 m to 10 m
  - Field of view: 270 degrees
  - Angular resolution: 0.33 degrees
  - Points per scan: 2,880
  - Scan frequency: 15 Hz
  - Laser: 850 nm (IR, Class 1, eye-safe)
  - Connector: M12 4-pin D-coded
  - Power: 9-28 VDC (separate connector)

Network:
  - Protocol: TCP/IP
  - Port: 2112
  - Format: COLA (CoLa A ASCII or CoLa B binary)
  - Checksum: CRC16-CCITT (polynomial 0x1021)
  - Default IP: 192.168.0.1 (DHCP with fallback)
  - Bandwidth: 135-225 KB/s @ 15 Hz

Security:
  - No public vulnerabilities found
  - Input validation implemented
  - MAC address verification (SICK OUI: 00:30:24)
  - Command whitelisting
  - Memory safety (ring buffers, size limits)
  - Keepalive recommended (10 sec)
  - Timeout: 5 seconds

Memory:
  - Ring buffer: ~92 KB (2 scans)
  - Telegram buffer: 4-64 KB (growable)
  - TCP sockets: ~24 KB (lwIP)
  - Driver structures: ~8 KB
  - Total typical: ~200 KB peak: ~300 KB
